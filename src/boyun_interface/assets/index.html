Scenario settings and their implemented effects
ParameterMeaningEffect / limit
schema_version · mode · nameFormat, synthetic execution mode, and a descriptive label.Keep synthetic-scenario.v1 and mode synthetic. Renaming does not change behavior; this engine does not accept an observed-market execution mode.
capital_cents · launch_cost_cents · lp_cash_centsStarting project cash, launch payment, and initial pool cash, in integer cents.More starting cash alone is not more profit. Costs reduce cash; pool cash changes reserves and stays locked. Capital must equal cast_recipe.founder_initial_cash_cents.
lp_tokens · founder_tokensInitial pool deposit and total initial project token inventory, in integer units.At fixed pool cash, more pool tokens lower the initial cash-per-token price. Pool allocation must not exceed founder inventory. Extra unsold tokens are excluded from cash profit.
horizon_hours · lp_unlock_at_hourResult cutoff and declared liquidity unlock hour.Longer horizons include more scheduled activity. Unlock must be strictly after the horizon: liquidity withdrawal is not implemented. The simple horizon form extends the lock if necessary.
founder_vested_at_hourEarliest absolute hour at which founder sales may execute.Later vesting can reject a scheduled early sale. Rejected sales are not retried automatically. This is not a gradual vesting schedule.
founder_sales[].at_hour · token_unitsSale time relative to launch, and token units offered.Larger sales can withdraw more cash while moving price and reducing remaining inventory. Events beyond the horizon do not run. A delay-launch plan shifts sale times by 24 hours; vesting remains an absolute-hour check.
campaign_budget_cents · campaigns[].fee_centsApproved spending ceiling and each scheduled post fee.In advanced JSON, raising the ceiling alone does not change fees or delivery. The simple budget form rescales existing fees. Fees are paid on publication; the engine does not buy reach or convert spend directly to buyers.
campaigns[].at_hourPost publication hour relative to launch.Earlier or later posts change which visits can see them and which news state they repost. More entries add posts, while higher fees on the same entries do not. Postponed launches shift this schedule 24 hours.
news_enabled · catalyst_probability_bpsEnable fixed news events and set the chance of initial confirmation.When enabled, hour 1 contains either confirmation or a rumor. Higher catalyst values increase confirmation frequency across worlds. A basis point is 0.01%; 6,500 means 65%.
followup_positive_probability_bpsSeparate assumed chance for favorable news at absolute hour 96.Favorable draws add a new confirmed follow-up origin; unfavorable draws contradict the initial catalyst. The draw is independent of initial confirmation. Horizons shorter than 96 hours omit it.
competitor_messages[].at_hourAbsolute publication hours of competing messages.More competing messages can consume subscribed actors’ limited feed attention. Actors do not trade a second competitor asset in this fixture. Adding text or an identity does not create an external news integration.
observation_window_hoursMaximum age of messages still available for first feed delivery.A larger window keeps old posts available longer; a smaller one can cause missed posts. It does not erase remembered claims: observed beliefs persist with no decay in this engine. Default: 48 hours.
visit_interval_hoursHours between actor observation and decision batches, starting at hour 0.Smaller intervals create more visits and computation; larger intervals may miss short-lived messages. At each visit an actor proposes at most one action. The whole batch quotes the same starting reserves before seeded execution.
fee_bpsSwap fee retained by the constant-product pool, from 0 to 9,999 bps.Higher fees reduce effective swap input and quoted output while retaining more in the pool. The net effect on founder cash is not guaranteed. Example: 30 bps = 0.30%.
max_slippage_bpsAllowed deterioration from each proposal’s start-of-visit output quote.Higher tolerance admits more fills after earlier trades move reserves; lower tolerance can reject them. It does not directly prevent a trade’s own price impact. This bound applies to participant proposals; scheduled founder sales use a one-cent minimum output.
assumptions[].id · status · rationaleHuman-readable provenance declarations retained with results.Clearer records improve interpretation and auditability; changing their text does not fit or validate numeric parameters. They must identify unmeasured behavior honestly.
Cast recipe fields — groups cover retail, event_traders, large_holders, liquidity, arbitrage, creators_marketing, launch_teams and analysts_community
ParameterMeaningEffect / limit
founder_count · founder_initial_cash_centsOne founder and the project’s starting cash.founder_count is fixed to 1. Cash must equal scenario capital; the simple capital control synchronizes both fields. The delegated LP shares the same project owner and adds no new cash.
two_executable_marketsWhether arbitrage roles are excluded for lack of two venues.False excludes arbitrage actors. True only retains observation roles: it does not implement a second market, routing or arbitrage trading.
role_templates.project-founder · project-lpReviewed description IDs reserved for the project roles.Changing them changes the chosen biography in the matching launch-team or liquidity category. It does not change scheduled founder behavior or implement extra LP actions.
groups.{category}.countNumber of individual actors requested per category.More retail, event-trader or large-holder actors add separately funded possible traders. Other categories remain passive or scheduled. Counts include founder/LP slots and cannot be interpreted as a representative market population.
groups.{category}.cash_range_centsInclusive lower and upper bounds for seeded initial actor cash.Higher bounds increase available outside cash; lower bounds reduce it. Each actor draws cash once when the cast is built. Greater cash does not force a purchase, and weight remains one actor per instance.
groups.{category}.position_limit_centsMaximum remaining cash cost basis allowed for token acquisition.A higher cap permits larger total acquisition cost basis; a lower cap restricts it. Partial sales reduce tracked cost basis proportionally, allowing capacity to be reused. This is not a limit on marked-to-market value or lifetime turnover.
groups.{category}.entry_fraction_bpsMaximum fraction of current cash for one entry.Higher values allow larger eligible purchases up to the remaining position limit; zero prevents entry. An explicit policy can only reduce this fraction. A funded actor still needs an eligible observed claim.
groups.{category}.attention_capacityMaximum eligible unseen messages delivered in one visit.More capacity can expose the actor to more subscribed claims or contradictions; zero prevents trading decisions in the current rules. It is not follower count or guaranteed engagement. Reposts of the same origin do not create fresh purchase opportunities.
groups.{category}.strategy_choicesSeeded choice of cautious or momentum for trading categories.Cautious enters on confirmation; momentum also accepts rumors. Nontrading categories accept observe only. Repeating a choice changes its sampling weight. These are fixed rule families, not independently reasoning agents.
groups.{category}.subscription_choicesSeeded choice among lists of source names.Adding news, creator or competitor changes which sources an actor can receive. Each list is sampled as one choice; repeating it changes its weight. Subscriptions do not fetch an external platform or establish a social relationship.
